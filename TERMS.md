The app records household expenses you enter manually. It provides monthly summaries, category and person breakdowns, bill charts, historical insights, CSV exports and annual PDF recaps where available in your version and access level.

Family profiles assign costs locally; they do not create separate user accounts or synchronize information across devices. Shared household expenses are organizational records, not transfers between family members.

Bills are entered with their actual amounts. The app does not pay bills, verify invoices, contact providers or guarantee reminders. It does not connect to banks, manage bank balances or confirm that a payment was made. The current version records expenses in euros; changing the interface language does not convert amounts.
