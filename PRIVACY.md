# Privacy Policy — Easy Budget Family

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## 1. Developer and contact

Easy Budget Family is an iPhone app developed by Light Cup Pix Lab, Padova, Italy. For privacy questions, contact **lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com**.

## 2. Household information stored in the app

You can enter expense descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, bill types, notes, a household name, and family profiles with names, icons and colors. The app uses this information to produce monthly totals, spending breakdowns, historical charts and annual recaps.

These records are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. There is no app account, developer-managed cloud storage or synchronization between family members' devices. A family profile is a local label for assigning expenses, not an account or invitation to another person.

The app does not connect to bank accounts, read payment cards, import bank transactions, or access Apple Health. You choose what to enter; avoid putting bank credentials, card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information in expense notes.

## 3. Tracking and device permissions

The app includes no advertising SDK, third-party analytics or developer-operated tracking service. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library for expense tracking.

## 4. Exports and backups

When you generate a CSV export or an annual PDF recap, the app creates a file on your device. If you use the iOS share sheet, you choose the recipient, application or storage provider. That destination's terms and privacy practices apply to the copy you share. Exported files may contain household names, expense details and amounts.

Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Such backups are separate from the app's own storage; the developer does not operate or access them. Copies you export, share or back up are not automatically removed when you delete records in the app.

## 5. Purchases and external links

If paid Pro access is enabled, Apple processes purchases for the monthly EasyBudgetFamily Pro subscription. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve products and check entitlement status. A local preference may cache whether Pro is active. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or run a subscription-verification backend.

Opening privacy, terms or support links connects you to an external service. Visiting a policy hosted on GitHub is subject to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Apple's handling of purchases and backups is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Expense tracking works with local records, but purchases, restoration and external websites can require an internet connection.

## 6. Access, correction and deletion

You can inspect and edit expenses and family profiles within the app. Where available, CSV and PDF exports provide copies of your information. In Settings, **Erase all data** removes the app's household records and resets household settings. This does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete previously shared files or device backups.

Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading it may retain data according to iOS behavior. Because the developer has no server-side copy of household records, the developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase them for you.

## 7. Security and retention

Local records use the app's iOS sandbox and file-protection facilities. Protect your device with a passcode and manage backups and exports carefully. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Records remain locally until you remove them or delete the app, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

## 8. Family profiles and support

An adult may use local profiles to organize expenses for children or other household members. These profiles do not transmit information about those people to the developer. Enter only information you are entitled to use.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include. We use it to answer and handle your request; do not attach private expense records unless needed. Support correspondence may be retained as needed for follow-up and applicable obligations.

## 9. Updates

This policy may be revised when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version. Contact us if you need clarification about the version you use.
